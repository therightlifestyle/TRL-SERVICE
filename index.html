The operational problem

When follow-up depends on memory, good enquiries can get lost.

Enquiries may arrive through WhatsApp, a website form, phone calls or walk-ins. If ownership and the next action are unclear, the clinic has little visibility into what is still waiting.

01 / ARRIVAL

Requests come through different doors

Staff need a simple way to see where an enquiry arrived and whether it has been picked up.

02 / OWNERSHIP

It is not always clear who responds

A named owner and a visible status make the handoff easier to manage.

03 / NEXT STEP

Follow-up can slip between busy moments

A next action and due date create a repeatable process without promising an outcome.

Illustrative operational scenarios only—not a claim about a specific clinic or measured result.

Boundaries that protect the clinic

Administrative systems. Clinical decisions stay with clinicians.

TRL does not diagnose, recommend treatment, interpret patient records or answer clinical questions. The design uses minimum-necessary information and keeps clinic staff in control.

HUMAN OWNERSHIP

Staff confirm appointments

The system can help log a request and make its next step visible. Clinic personnel confirm the appointment and decide what to tell the patient.

NO OUTCOME PROMISES

No guaranteed bookings or revenue

TRL promises a defined process and agreed deliverables—not an increase in patients, appointments or income.
