THE STANDARD

Be useful. Be specific. Be honest.

Start from the actual workflow, not a tool or buzzword. Write down what is included and excluded. Keep clinic staff responsible for clinical decisions. Hand over a system the team can understand and own.

TRL has not yet delivered the active offer for a real clinic. There are no client case studies or measured results to claim. The public demonstrations are labelled and use fictional data.

What TRL will not compromise

Clear boundaries are part of the service.

01

No invented proof

No fabricated clients, testimonials, statistics, logos or outcome claims.

02

No clinical advice

Clinical questions belong with qualified clinic staff, not a workflow tool.

03

No hidden scope

Price, scope, exclusions, responsibilities and acceptance are written down before work begins.

One focused next step

Describe the workflow, not the software.

We can start with how enquiries arrive and what happens next.
