How delivery works

Assessment → implementation → monthly management.

The assessment is the starting point. It identifies what is happening before a fix is scoped. Implementation and ongoing management are separate, written decisions.

01

Assessment

Map enquiry channels, current handoffs, visible follow-up gaps and the clinic’s constraints. The output is a written assessment and prioritised plan.

02

Implementation

Configure the selected modules in the agreed tools, test them with clinic staff, document the operating steps, and train the people who use them.

03

Monthly management

Monitor, maintain, report and review only the agreed items, within the hours, response target and change limit in the signed agreement.

No work starts on a vague promise. The proposal states scope, exclusions, responsibilities, price, target dates and acceptance criteria in writing. Required advance payment must be received before implementation begins.

Non-negotiable healthcare boundary

Clinic staff keep clinical responsibility.

TRL DOES NOT

Provide clinical advice

No diagnosis, treatment recommendations, patient triage, clinical-record interpretation, billing or insurance workflows. Clinical questions are routed to qualified clinic personnel.

DATA MINIMISATION

Use patient information for demos

Patient data is not copied into TRL documents, demonstrations or reports. Any access requires the clinic’s written authorisation and is limited to what the agreed work needs.

NO GUARANTEED OUTCOMES

Promise more patients or revenue

TRL does not guarantee patient counts, appointments, revenue, conversion rates or any percentage improvement.

OUT OF SCOPE

Silently add extra work

Paid third-party software, hosting, messaging fees, advertising, custom software development, unlimited support and unlisted work require separate written agreement.

Scope and practical questions

What to know before a conversation.

How much does the system cost?
No public price is approved. A price is quoted in writing for a named clinic after the workflow and proposed modules are understood. Third-party costs are separate and identified before commitment.
How long does the work take?
The current internal target is assessment within three working days after the advance and complete information, and standard implementation within ten working days. These are scope-dependent targets, not guarantees; the written quote controls.
Is AI included?
The active offer is not an AI product. Any automation is considered only where it removes a specific named administrative step and is approved in scope. AI must not answer clinical questions or replace clinic judgement.
What does the clinic need to provide?
The clinic supplies an authorised point of contact, a description of its current process, decisions about who owns each step, and access only to the tools and data agreed in writing. Patient information is minimised.
Who owns the system at handover?
The engagement is designed around clinic-owned accounts and tools. The specific ownership, access, documentation and handover items are written into the proposal before work begins.
Has TRL delivered this offer for a real clinic yet?
Not yet. There are no clinic case studies, testimonials, measured improvements or client results to claim. The site’s examples are explicitly fictional demonstrations.
