Privacy notice

Last reviewed 7 November 2026. This notice describes the current static website and local demonstration—not a deployed client system.

Do not submit patient data. The interactive demo is fictional workflow planning only. It is not a clinic system and must not contain patient names, contact details, records, photos, symptoms or health information.

Information on this website

The static pages do not require an account. The workflow demonstration runs in your browser and does not send its inputs to TRL or an AI provider. The optional light/dark theme preference may be stored in your browser’s local storage. There are no third-party advertising trackers in this draft.

When you contact TRL

The contact form prepares a message for WhatsApp or email. It does not submit anything automatically. Your message is sent only if you choose the destination and press Send in WhatsApp or your email app. Those services then process the message under their own terms and privacy policies.

What to include

Share only generic operational information needed to discuss the clinic’s enquiry workflow. Do not send patient-identifiable or clinical information through this website or the interactive demonstration.

Future integrations

If TRL adds hosted lead storage, AI, analytics, or other services later, this notice must be updated before those features are enabled to identify what is collected, which providers receive it, retention, and the choices available to visitors.
